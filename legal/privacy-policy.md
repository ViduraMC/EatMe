# EatMe Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

EatMe is a mobile app that connects people and businesses with surplus food
(donors) to charities, orphanages and elders' homes that need it (recipients),
with coordinators and volunteer drivers arranging the delivery. This policy
explains what personal information the app collects, why, and how you can have
it deleted.

Contact: **SUPPORT_EMAIL**

## What we collect

When you create an account or use the app we store:

- **Account details:** your name, email address, phone number, address, the
  role you signed up as, and, for organisations, the organisation name.
- **Your password,** stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. Nobody, including us,
  can read the original password.
- **Activity in the app:** the food listings you post (titles, descriptions,
  quantities, expiry times, pickup address and pickup window), the food requests
  you make and the notes you add to them, and the delivery records that link a
  listing, a request and a driver, including handover confirmations and their
  times.

We do **not** collect your location from your phone (GPS), contacts, photos,
camera, microphone, advertising ID or payment details. The app has no adverts
and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Why we use it

Only to run the service: to let you sign in, to show donors' food to recipient
organisations, to let a coordinator assign a driver, and to let both sides
confirm a handover. Your name, organisation and phone number are shown to the
other people involved in a donation you take part in (the donor, the recipient
organisation, the coordinator and the driver) so the food can be collected and
delivered. Administrators can see account details to approve organisations and
keep the platform safe.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else, except where the
law requires it.

## Where it is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database run by our hosting providers (Neon and
Render, in their Singapore data centres), who process it only on our
instructions. All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with
HTTPS. Your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure keystore (Android
Keystore), and every request is checked against your account's role.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is open. When you delete
your account (below) your personal details are removed straight away.

## Deleting your account

**In the app (donors and recipient organisations):** open **Profile → Delete
account** and confirm with your password. Your name, email, phone number,
address and organisation name are erased immediately, your open listings and
pending requests are cancelled, and you can no longer sign in. Records of
donations that were already delivered are kept without your personal details,
because the other organisations involved rely on that history.

**Without the app, or for staff accounts (coordinators and drivers):** email
**SUPPORT_EMAIL** from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my
EatMe account". We will delete it in the same way within 30 days.

## Children

EatMe is for adults acting for themselves or for an organisation. It is not
directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their
information.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant
changes, tell you in the app.
